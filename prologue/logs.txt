LOG 001:1995-03-12
Today marks the day my loneliness has ended. I’d been travelling for hours and I stumbled upon a Dissonant Spirit of Rage. Honestly, if it wasn’t lying on the ground, crying, I would’ve left it alone and ran away. I can’t neglect something in need when I’m the only one around to do things, especially since I’ve been without help myself for years. The streets of San’ya had been negligent toward my situation.
===
LOG 002:1995-03-16
It’s been four days since I met this spirit. When I met her, I offered to travel with her, and it led to now. She’d asked for my name and I asked for hers. She told me she didn’t have one. And so, I gave her a name. Shizue Kaori. Ever since then, we’ve been travelling for a while. Travelling in search of a decent supply of food and water. San’ya was out of the question if I wanted help from people.
===
LOG 003:1995-03-17
Earlier today, we made it to Shinjuku, Tokyo. Now, by then, we finally decided to stop travelling around for a while. We were tired of aimlessly exploring Tokyo. And so we settled down in Shinjuku. Shizue ended up asking me why I’d help her despite her being a dissonant spirit. I didn’t give her a clear answer, I mostly ended up consoling her. I never thought I’d come across a spirit like this, but anything is possible at this point.
===
LOG 004:1995-03-18
Before the sun came up, Shizue woke me up and hid somewhere nearby. Before I could react, a few people had come up to me and had given me a rice cake to eat. Before they left, I asked for another one for Shizue, but they denied me the extra rice cake and left. Once they left, Shizue came out from hiding. I gave her half of my rice cake and we ended up leaving Shinjuku. From that point on, I finally set myself on a task. To get a job in order to get an actual house for me and her.
===
LOG 005:1995-03-25
After countless attempts, I’d finally got a job. It was at a hostess bar in Kabukicho, where I’d have to clean up during closing hours. Shizue didn’t like the idea of me working at a hostess bar at the time for some reason, but it was the only job I could manage to get in. I told her that whenever I clocked in, she should hide nearby somewhere to prevent the chosen people from hunting her down. There are very few chosen every 4 years, but I don’t want to take the chance. I can’t take the chance. I start working at the hostess bar tomorrow.
===
LOG 006:1996-08-01
It’s been a long time since my last log… I had forgotten about this for a while. Let me catch you up with everything. Around July of 1995, I won the lottery. 160m yen. With this money, I was able to buy a great house in the outskirts of Tokyo and find myself a great job. Shizue was safe from now on, and I could make a lot more than the job I had before. Life felt amazing during that time… and then… Just last month… Shizue Kaori had confessed her feelings to me after so long. From that, a healthy relationship had formed. It felt like I had the world in the palm of my hand after so long of suffering… And, that’s all I wanted to touch up on. I’d rather leave out the parts that weren’t so great during that time.
===
LOG 007:1996-08-05
Sometimes I wonder if a standard future with her would even be possible. I just… wanted to say that before going to bed.

LOG 008:1996-08-06
Shizue wants me to spend more time with her rather than me using some of my free time to write things. Though, I can’t throw away this logbook. I may return to write periodically, but it could be a while between logs.
===
LOG 009:1997-05-02
I can’t… I can’t believe it. She proposed to me… Shizue… We’re married… But… since she’s a spirit, there won’t be a wedding… Screw it, things are moving pretty fast anyway, so what if there’s a few things skipped? To think that just 3 years ago, I was homeless and alone…
===
LOG 010:1999-03-12
So it’s been a while, and… My daughter was born today. So many name suggestions from Shizue… I can’t express how I felt when I heard her, genuinely excited, spitting out names like gum. After a long while of name deciding, we settled on a name. “Suzume Kaori”. I guess a future with Shizue was really possible, Tsuneo from LOG 007.
===
LOG 011:2007-04-08
Tsuneo… My sweet, sweet Tsuneo… I came back across this book… I miss you so much. Ever since that day, 3 years ago, I’ve been in eternal despair… Since the day you died… If only we blended in enough with the rest of society more… Then my little Suzume wouldn’t have been taken that day… I still hate myself so much for running away from it all. I just wanted to have a life with my husband and daughter… And so if I can’t have my Suzume… I’m going to have someone else’s kid who was chosen by Zettai Sentaku to get back at them… I managed to kidnap one of them… I miss you so much, Tsuneo…
